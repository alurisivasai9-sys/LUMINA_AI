# LUMINA AI - Research Notes

## 1. Project

LUMINA AI is a prototype climate-skin intelligence platform designed
for the L'Oréal Sustainability Challenge 2026.

The project is aligned with:

Option 02 - Longevity and Future Aging for GenZ in the Era of
Climate Change and Pollution.

---

## 2. Problem

Gen Z consumers in India are exposed to different environmental
conditions including:

- UV exposure
- Air pollution
- Heat
- Humidity
- Outdoor exposure
- Urban environmental stress

Traditional skincare routines do not necessarily adapt to these
changing environmental conditions.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

LUMINA AI combines:

1. User profile
2. Environmental conditions
3. Outdoor exposure
4. Machine learning
5. Explainable recommendations

The system generates a prototype:

Climate Skin Stress Score

The score ranges from:

0 - 100

Higher scores indicate higher estimated environmental stress.

---

## 4. Input Variables

The prototype currently uses:

- UV Index
- PM2.5
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Outdoor Exposure
- Age
- Skin Type
- Activity Level

---

## 5. Machine Learning

The prototype compares:

- Linear Regression
- Random Forest Regression
- Gradient Boosting Regression

The model with the lowest Mean Absolute Error (MAE) is selected.

Evaluation metrics:

- MAE
- RMSE
- R²

---

## 6. Important Scientific Limitation

The current dataset is synthetic.

The dataset is intended only for:

- ML pipeline development
- Software demonstration
- Competition prototype
- Model experimentation

It must not be presented as clinical evidence.

A real deployment would require:

- Longitudinal consumer data
- Environmental exposure measurements
- Skin measurements
- Controlled studies
- Clinical validation
- Appropriate ethical and privacy safeguards

---

## 7. Sustainability Direction

Potential sustainability directions include:

- Personalized product selection
- Avoiding unnecessary product use
- Refillable packaging
- Responsible ingredient sourcing
- Water-conscious product formats
- Concentrated formulations
- Reduced product waste

These should be evaluated with actual lifecycle data before making
quantitative sustainability claims.

## 9. Prototype Disclaimer

LUMINA AI is a competition prototype.

The Climate Skin Stress Score is not a medical diagnosis,
disease prediction, or clinical assessment.

The current model uses synthetic data and requires real-world
validation before any consumer or clinical deployment.
